Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy transparently explains how DANSCOM, owned and operated by Daniel Musembi ("we", "us", or "our"), treats information when you browse this website (hosted on GitHub Pages) and contact us.

1. Pure Static Site Architecture & Zero Tracking Cookies

This website is built as a pure static web platform. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies, Google Analytics, telemetry trackers, or invasive session recorders.

2. Local Storage & Service Worker Caching

To enhance your browsing experience, this website uses browser Local Storage and a Progressive Web App (PWA) Service Worker strictly for:

  • Theme Preference: Storing whether you prefer dark mode or light mode (danscom_theme).
  • Offline Functionality: Caching static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG icons locally on your device so the site continues to operate during intermittent or offline connections.

This data remains exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

3. Hosting & Server Logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (a service provided by GitHub, Inc.). When you access the site, GitHub servers may automatically log basic technical request details, including:

  • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring website address

These logs are processed by GitHub for security, network diagnostics, and abuse prevention according to GitHub’s own Privacy Statement. We do not have direct access to private individual IP records.

4. Inquiries & Voluntary Communications

When you contact Daniel Musembi via the contact form, email (musembidaniel615@gmail.com), or messaging platforms:

  • The contact form does not store submissions in a public database. It constructs an email addressed directly to Daniel Musembi.
  • Your name, email address, and inquiry message are used solely to review your project scope and correspond with you directly.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your contact information with marketers or third-party data brokers.

5. Third-Party Links & External Platforms

This portfolio may contain links to external platforms (such as GitHub, LinkedIn, TradingView, or Deriv). We are not responsible for the privacy policies or content of external sites. We encourage you to review their terms upon visiting.
