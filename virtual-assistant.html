Core Service Offering

Executive Virtual Assistance & Operational Precision

Unburden yourself from operational friction. I manage your daily administrative workflows, correspondence, scheduling, and specialized industry coordination with uncompromising accuracy and discretion.

  • Outdated or duplicate records corrupting your CRM and lead pipeline.
  • Real estate listings delayed by missing paperwork or unformatted media.

Core Deliverables & Capability Spectrum

1. Executive Communications & Inbox Management

I triage your incoming mail stream using customized tagging rules (Urgent, Action Required, Waiting on Response, Reference). Routine communications are handled with professional templates or draft responses ready for your one-click approval, eliminating email anxiety.

2. Calendar Scheduling & Meeting Coordination

Cross-timezone calendar scheduling, buffer time protection, Zoom/Google Meet link verification, and preparation of contextual participant dossiers prior to executive calls.

3. Spreadsheet Data Hygiene & Analysis

Structuring, cleaning, and validating business datasets in Google Sheets or Excel. Expertise in VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, pivot tables, deduplication routines, and conditional formatting to produce audit-ready financial and operational logs.

4. Real Estate Specialist Support

Dedicated back-office support for realtors, property brokers, and landlords:

  • Preparing and organizing listing packets, property disclosures, and title docs.
  • Inputting and cross-checking listing information across MLS and web portals.
  • Scheduling client property viewings and managing showing feedback logs.
  • Tracking escrow deadlines, inspection contingencies, and signature statuses.

5. E-Commerce & Customer Assistance

Catalog maintenance, product description updates, order status tracking, inventory reconciliation, and courteous customer support responses across email and ticket queues.

6. Lead Research & CRM Updates

Conducting internet and LinkedIn research to identify decision-makers, verify business contact info, and maintain pristine records in HubSpot, Trello, Notion, or Airtable.
