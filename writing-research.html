Intel & Synthesis

Online Writing & Investigative Intelligence

In an era of generic AI fluff, high-signal information is the ultimate competitive advantage. I synthesize deep web research, primary public documentation, and clear technical prose into decision-grade assets.

The Criminological Advantage in Research

Holding a Bachelor of Arts in Criminology and Penology from Maasai Mara University (2019–2023), I apply formal evidentiary standards to digital research. Rather than accepting superficial marketing claims, I verify corporate filings, examine historical domain changes, cross-reference public registries, and cite primary sources with academic discipline.

Core Research & Writing Deliverables

1. Deep-Dive Market & Competitor Intelligence

Comprehensive competitive landscape audits analyzing market positioning, feature parity, pricing structures, customer complaints on public forums, and historical trajectory. Delivered as structured executive briefings with risk matrices.

2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) & Internal Documentation

Documenting institutional knowledge into clear, step-by-step SOPs. I turn chaotic workflows into clean checklists and guides that allow new team members or virtual assistants to execute tasks flawlessly.

3. Article Rewriting & Content Polishing

Transforming awkward, generic, or AI-generated drafts into crisp, authoritative prose with active voice, coherent structural flow, and factual verification.

4. Due Diligence & Background Dossiers

Open-source intelligence (OSINT) research on prospective partners, corporate entities, vendor reputations, and regulatory compliance histories.

5. Technical Summaries & Whitepaper Synthesis

Distilling 50-page regulatory statutes, academic papers, or software specifications into concise 2-page executive summaries highlighting key takeaways, dates, and operational implications.

The 4-Step Client Research Workflow

01. Scope Definition

We clarify the core research hypothesis, target entities, required data points, and acceptable source tiers (peer-reviewed, government filings, verified news).

02. Source Triangulation

Data is harvested across academic databases, public registries, web archives, and technical documentation, cross-verifying each fact across multiple independent nodes.

03. Synthesis & Analysis

Unstructured notes are distilled into clean tables, executive summaries, annotated bibliographies, and decision recommendations.

04. Delivery & Revisions

Deliverables provided in Markdown, Google Docs, or PDF format with clickable reference links and one round of scope refinement.

Originality & Attribution: All writing is 100% human-crafted and edited. Zero plagiarized content, fabricated quotes, or unattributed citations.
