Pragmatic AI Workflows & Process Automation

Moving beyond trivial chatbots to construct dependable, deterministic automation pipelines. I help businesses harness LLMs and webhook connections to eliminate manual bottlenecks without technical debt.

Honest Architecture: Static Capabilities vs. External Backend Requirements

At DANSCOM, I uphold total architectural honesty. We clearly distinguish between what can be delivered as lightweight client-side tools and what requires secure backend microservices or third-party webhooks:

Delivered Directly / Zero-Backend
  • Client-side prompt engineering frameworks and templates.
  • Static PWA tools with offline data storage (LocalStorage / IndexedDB).
  • Browser-based text formatting, regex cleanup, and CSV transformation.
  • Comprehensive automation system audits and implementation roadmaps.
Requires External APIs / Managed Webhooks
  • Live Zapier, Make.com, or n8n multi-step webhooks.
  • Direct OpenAI / Gemini server-side API proxy routes (keeps keys secure).
  • Continuous background polling or scheduled cron worker servers.
  • Live database synchronizations (PostgreSQL / Firebase / Supabase).

Core Automation Services

1. Structured Prompt Architectures & Synthesis

Standard chat queries often produce vague, hallucinated answers. I design multi-shot prompt templates enforcing strict JSON Schema formatting, explicit negative constraints, and contextual references. This ensures AI outputs are reliable enough to feed directly into spreadsheets, CRM records, or drafting pipelines.

2. Inbound Lead Qualification & Triage Workflows

When inquiries arrive, an automated webhook pipeline extracts key criteria (project budget, timeline, industry), queries verification sources, and routes the lead into categorized CRM tiers, drafting customized follow-up emails for human sign-off.

3. Document Summarization & Extraction Pipelines

Converting dense PDFs, real estate inspection reports, transcripts, or meeting recordings into 1-page executive decision memos with action items, key dates, and risk flags.

4. Chatbot Blueprints & Customer Service FAQ Engines

Designing conversational support architecture with boundary constraints to prevent unauthorized promises, hallucinated pricing, or sensitive data leakage.

5. Personal & Executive Productivity System Design

Structuring Notion workspaces, Trello Kanban boards, and Google Drive tagging systems integrated with automated recurring reminders and template generators.

Human-in-the-Loop Principle

Zero Unsupervised Automation: In high-stakes business operations, full autonomy is a liability. DANSCOM builds systems where automation performs 90% of the extraction and drafting heavy lifting, while a qualified human (or your designated team member) provides final review before any email is dispatched or contract is altered.
